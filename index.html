How to play

Beat the dealer without going over 21. Number cards count at face value; J, Q and K count as 10; A counts as 1 or 11. Dealer stands on 17. Blackjack pays 3:2. Chips are fictional and have no cash value.

This friends-only prototype syncs room state through Firestore. It is not cheat-proof: players in a room may be able to tamper with shared game state. Never use real money or valuable stakes.

Firebase setup

The game runs in offline demo mode until you add your Firebase web-app configuration. Follow the setup guide in the repository README.
